# Alongway — Decisions Log

_From founding conversation: April 10-11, 2026 (late night session)_

## Confirmed Decisions

### Name & Entity
- **Name:** Alongway ("bolsa" = bag in Spanish, "made" = custom/crafted)
- **LLC:** Orange Collective (existing)
- **DBA:** Alongway (new filing needed)
- **Shares:** Same QuickBooks, EIN, seller's permit as OG
- **Domain:** alongway.com (appears available)
- **Instagram:** @alongway (needs claiming)

### Product
- **13 styles** (see product lineup doc):
  - Core: Essential, Carry Tote, Contrast Carry, Shoulder Tote, Oversized Tote, Flat Tote, Mini Tote
  - Specialty: Boat Tote, Carry-All (IKEA style), Tyvek Zip Tote (Aloha style), Wine Tote
  - Premium: Dopp Kit, Weekender
- **Styles are defined by shape, NOT fabric** — fabric is a separate choice/upgrade
- **Fully custom option:** Not at launch. Add later when business has momentum. MOQ would be 100.

### Fabrics
- **Standard (included):** Cotton canvas, cotton twill, ripstop
- **Weight upgrades:** 12oz (+$0.50), 16oz (+$1.00), 20oz (+$2.00), 24oz (+$4.00)
- **Add-on fabrics:** Corduroy, denim, washed canvas, organic cotton, nylon ripstop, camo

### Decoration
- **Included:** 1-color screen print OR embroidery (under 6")
- **Screen print and embroidery same price** if embroidery stays under 6"
- **Over 6" embroidery:** Add-on priced by size

### Labels
- **Custom woven label included** — 3 placement options:
  1. Flag label (side seam)
  2. Fold label (top seam)
  3. Sewn-on label (4 corners, inside or outside)
- **Alongway care label** on every bag: brand name, fabric content, alongway.com, "Custom Made"
- **No other Alongway branding on the bag** — fully branded for the client

### Pricing
- **Transparent, on the website** — no "request a quote" games
- **Priced by style × fabric weight × quantity tier**
- **Add-ons visible with clear pricing**
- **All-in:** shipping, setup, label, decoration all included in base price

### Order Minimums
- **50 pieces minimum per style**
- **100 pieces minimum per order**
- Mix and match styles to hit 100

### Samples & Swatches
- **Swatch cards:** 4"×4" fabric squares, labeled with weight/type, stapled to branded Alongway card
- **Basic swatch kit:** Free or $5 (standard fabrics only)
- **Full swatch kit:** $15-20 (all fabrics + weights)
- **Individual sample:** $15 (any style in natural canvas)
- **Full sample pack:** $50-75 (one of each style)
- **Sample inventory:** Hold 50-100 of each style in natural canvas at office

### Factory
- **HB Findo (Emily)** — primary manufacturer
- **Production:** 50 units is doable but costs more per unit
- **Timeline:** 30 days production + 10-15 days shipping

### OG Relationship
- **Not a priority to define right now** — focus on building Alongway as its own thing
- Shared CRM and contacts (HubSpot)
- Care label serves as organic marketing
- Cross-promotion can happen naturally later
- "It really doesn't matter at this stage" — Easton

### Automation
- **Goal:** As automated as possible
- Jenna handles most communication since variables are known and limited
- Easton/Josh needed for tech packs, mockups, design review
- Easton available for expert calls and emails when needed
- Standardized process means less back-and-forth per order

### Messaging
- "We set out to make the best and most customizable totes through a simple, fast, affordable, and fully branded process"
- "There are plenty of totes out there. None like Alongway."
- Four pillars: Simple, Fast, Affordable, Fully Branded
