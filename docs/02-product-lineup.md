## How It Works

Every Alongway bag includes:
- Choice of silhouette
- Choice of standard fabric (cotton canvas, cotton twill, ripstop)
- Choice of fabric weight (base weight per style, upgrades available)
- Choice of strap/handle color
- Choice of stitch color
- 1-color screen print OR embroidery (included — embroidery under 6" at no extra charge)
- Custom woven label (included — 3 placement options)
- Shipping (included, sea freight standard)
- Setup fees ($0)
- Alongway care label inside every bag (alongway.com — the lead generator)

No hidden costs. The price is the price.

## The Styles

### CORE TOTES
_Standard fabric: 10oz cotton canvas. The bread and butter._

#### 1. The Carry Tote
The standard. The go-to. The one most people order. Based on the AS Colour 1001 silhouette.
- *Dimensions:* 16.54"W × 16.54"H, Gusset 3.74", Straps 28.35"
- *Handles:* Self-fabric, comfortable carry length
- *Best for:* Everyone — coffee shops, retail, startups, events, corporate
- *Why it exists:* 60% of orders will probably be this bag. The universal tote. The starting point for any brand that wants a quality custom tote.

#### 2. The Shoulder Tote
Slightly larger than The Carry, with longer handles for comfortable over-shoulder carry.
- *Dimensions:* 18.11"W × 14.57"H, Gusset 4.92", Straps 23.62"
- *Handles:* Longer, over-shoulder length
- *Best for:* Everyday carry, shopping, work bags, farmers markets
- *Why it exists:* Some people want a bit more room and the ability to sling it over a shoulder. Fills that gap.

#### 3. The Oversized Tote
The big one. AS Colour 1040 energy. Roomy, substantial, statement bag.
- *Dimensions:* 26.77"W × 14.57"H, Gusset 18.11" × 7.89", Straps 23.62"
- *Handles:* Over-shoulder length, wider straps
- *Best for:* Bookstores, beach brands, retail, lifestyle, farmers markets, "carry everything" use cases
- *Why it exists:* When a regular tote isn't enough. The bag people grab when they're going out for the day.

#### 4. The Flat Tote
No gusset. Minimalist. Clean. Oak Essentials / Aesop vibes.
- *Dimensions:* 13.97"W × 15.35"H, Straps 27.55"
- *Handles:* Shoulder-length, flat self-fabric
- *Best for:* Beauty brands, luxury and minimalist brands, fashion, art galleries, high-end retail
- *Why it exists:* For brands with taste. Looks great in a flat lay, hangs clean off a shoulder. Budget tier.

#### 5. The Mini Tote
Small. Gift bags, swag bags, accessories, lunch. Trader Joe's energy.
- *Dimensions:* 8"W × 8"H × 1.5"D, Handles 14"
- *Handles:* Short hand-carry
- *Best for:* Events, gifting, retail packaging, swag, lunch bags, cosmetics
- *Why it exists:* Perfect for gift sets, event bags, or retail packaging upgrades. Customers reuse them — that's the whole point.

---

### SPECIALTY TOTES

#### 6. The Boat Tote
Heavy canvas, structured, boxy. The heritage/classic tote. L.L. Bean energy. Available in 4 sizes.
- *Default fabric:* 24oz heavy canvas
- *Handles:* Short hand-carry (Regular) + optional long shoulder (Long), reinforced
- *Sizes:*
  - Mini: 10"W × 7"H × 6"D — Regular 5", Long 14"
  - Small: 9.5"W × 10.5"H × 5"D — approx. 504 cu in / 8L — Regular 5", Long 14"
  - Medium: 13"W × 12"H × 6"D — approx. 1,008 cu in / 17L — Regular 6", Long 14"
  - Large: 17"W × 15"H × 7.5"D — approx. 2,124 cu in / 35L — Regular 8", Long 14"
  - Extra-Large: 19"W × 17"H × 10"D — approx. 3,400 cu in / 56L — Regular 10", Long 14"
- *Best for:* Nautical, preppy, yacht clubs, premium gifting, hospitality, heritage brands
- *Why it exists:* The heavyweight. Built to last decades. This is the bag people fight over at estate sales. Premium tier.

#### 7. The Puffer Tote
Quilted and padded — the fashion-forward bag. Summer Fridays energy.
- *Dimensions:* 16.5"W × 13"H × 6"D, Handles 10"
- *Material:* Quilted fabric with padding
- *Handles:* Short hand-carry
- *Best for:* Beauty brands, fashion, lifestyle, DTC brands, luxury gifting
- *Why it exists:* Trending hard right now. The puffer texture is a visual statement on its own. High perceived value.

#### 8. The Market Mesh Tote
Open-weave mesh construction with a branded printed pocket and woven label.
- *Material:* Open mesh + printed pocket combo
- *Best for:* Grocery brands, produce, farmers markets, beach and outdoor, eco-focused brands
- *Why it exists:* Breathable, lightweight, and completely different from canvas. The mesh + pocket combo gives it a practical, market-ready feel without looking cheap.

#### 9. The Side Pocket Tote
Classic tote silhouette with an exterior side pocket — functional upgrade.
- *Best for:* Any brand that wants utility — phone pocket, keys, water bottle, quick access
- *Why it exists:* The one complaint about standard totes is "nowhere to put your phone." Solved.

#### 10. The Panel Tote
Structured tote with a defined front panel — great for large graphic placement.
- *Best for:* Brands with strong visual identity, artwork, or photography
- *Why it exists:* Some designs need more real estate. The panel creates a canvas for big, clean prints.

### UTILITY / CARRY

#### 11. The Carry-All
The IKEA FRAKTA in disguise. Massive, woven polypropylene, indestructible.
- *Material:* Woven polypropylene (different from standard fabrics)
- *Handles:* Dual — short grab + long shoulder
- *Best for:* Moving companies, retail, trade shows, hardware and outdoor brands, events where you're handing out a lot of stuff
- *Why it exists:* Sometimes you need a bag that holds everything and takes a beating. Dirt cheap to produce, massive capacity, instantly recognizable silhouette.

#### 12. The Tyvek Zip Tote
Aloha Collection vibes. Tyvek material, zippered closure, water-resistant, packable.
- *Material:* Tyvek (lightweight, water-resistant, unique crinkle texture)
- *Closure:* Zipper
- *Handles:* Standard carry length
- *Best for:* Surf and beach brands, travel companies, fitness, outdoor, hospitality — anything near water
- *Why it exists:* Tyvek is a differentiator. It looks and feels completely different from canvas. Water-resistant, lightweight, and that papery crinkle texture makes people ask "what is this made of?" Great for LA-adjacent brands.

#### 13. The Wine Tote
Bottle carrier. 2 or 4 bottle format.
- *Shape:* Tall, narrow, divided compartments
- *Handles:* Short hand-carry or shoulder length options
- *Best for:* Wineries, breweries, restaurants, tequila brands, coffee roasters, food & bev gifting, weddings
- *Why it exists:* Every food & bev brand needs one. Year-round demand. High gift appeal.

---

### PREMIUM / ACCESSORY

#### 14. The Dopp Kit
Zippered accessory and toiletry bag.
- *Shape:* Rectangular, zippered, structured
- *Best for:* Corporate gifting, employee onboarding kits, travel brands, hotels, hospitality
- *Why it exists:* Not a tote, but every company doing welcome kits or gift boxes wants one. High perceived value, great margins.

#### 15. The Weekender
Duffle / overnight bag. The premium showpiece.
- *Shape:* Large duffle, structured bottom
- *Handles:* Dual carry handles + detachable shoulder strap
- *Closure:* Zipper
- *Default fabric:* 16oz+ canvas (waxed canvas option available)
- *Best for:* Premium gifting, hospitality, travel brands, luxury merch, executive gifts, employee onboarding
- *Why it exists:* The top of the line. This is the bag companies give to VIPs, new hires, or top-tier clients. Not a tote — a statement.

## Launch Recommendation

15 styles is a strong, comprehensive lineup. Two ways to approach it:

*Option A — Launch all 15:*
Positions Alongway as the most complete custom bag shop from day one. More styles = more reasons to buy. Downside: more photography, more sample inventory, more complexity.

*Option B — Launch 12, expand later:*
Launch the 12 most distinct styles. Hold The Carry-All, The Tyvek Zip Tote, and one other for "Season 2" drops a few months in. Those two are niche enough that their own launch moment would get more attention than being buried in a 15-style lineup.

Recommendation: Launch 12 strong ones. Drop Carry-All + Tyvek Zip as "New Style" moments on IG 2-3 months in. It gives you content cadence and keeps the initial product page focused.
