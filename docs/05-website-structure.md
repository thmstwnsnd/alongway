# Alongway — Website Structure

_Start with a focused one-pager. Expand as the business grows._

## Domain
**alongway.com** (appears available — register ASAP)

## Platform Options
1. **Squarespace** — Cleanest templates, easiest to maintain, great for visual brands. Tapacho uses it.
2. **Webflow** — More control, better for custom layouts, slightly steeper learning curve.
3. **Shopify** — If we want e-commerce/cart functionality later (ordering totes online).
4. **Custom (Next.js)** — Full control but overkill for launch. Save for later.

**Recommendation:** Squarespace to start. Fast to launch, looks professional, easy for Easton to update. Switch to custom build later if needed.

---

## Phase 1: Launch Site (One-Pager + Key Pages)

### Homepage (The One-Pager)

The homepage should tell the full story in one scroll. Someone lands here and within 30 seconds knows:
1. What Alongway does
2. How it works
3. What it costs
4. How to get started

**Section flow:**

#### Hero
- Bold headline: **"Custom Bags, Made Simple."**
- Subhead: "Pick your style. Choose your fabric. We handle everything else."
- CTA: "See Our Bags" (scrolls to lineup) + "Get a Quote" (jumps to form)
- Background: lifestyle shot of someone carrying a custom Alongway tote OR a flat lay of multiple styles

#### How It Works (3 Steps)
1. **Pick Your Style** — Choose from 10 curated silhouettes, from mini totes to weekenders
2. **Customize Everything** — Fabric, color, handles, print or embroidery, woven label — all included
3. **We Make It** — 30-day production, shipped to your door. That's it.

#### The Lineup (Product Grid)
- 10 tote thumbnails in a grid
- Each card: photo, name, starting price ("From $7.50"), brief description
- Click/tap → expands to full style detail (dimensions, fabrics, use cases)
- This is the catalog page, right on the homepage

#### Pricing
- Clean tier table (50 / 100 / 250 / 500 / 1,000)
- "Standard" and "Premium" categories
- What's included checklist
- Add-ons list with prices
- **The customer should know their cost in 10 seconds**

#### Social Proof / Gallery
- Client logos (once we have them — start with OG clients who ordered totes)
- Product gallery: real photos + mockups
- 1-2 testimonials (can use OG tote testimonials initially)

#### About
- 2-3 sentences: who we are, LA-based, part of the Orange Goods family
- Photo of Easton or the team
- "We've made thousands of custom products for brands like Stanford, Verve Coffee, Red Bull, and more. Now we're bringing that same quality and service to one thing: bags."

#### Get Started (Form)
Keep the form simple:
- Name
- Company / Brand
- Email
- Phone (optional)
- Which style(s) are you interested in? (checkboxes of the 10 styles + "Not sure / Custom")
- Quantity (dropdown: 50 / 100 / 250 / 500 / 1,000+)
- Tell us about your project (open text)
- CTA: "Let's Make Your Bag"

#### Footer
- Contact: hello@alongway.com
- Social: @alongway (IG)
- "Part of the Orange Goods family" with small OG logo
- LA, California

### Bag Guide Page (Like Tapacho's Hat Guide)
- Each of the 10 styles with:
  - Large product photo/mockup
  - Dimensions diagram
  - Fabric options for that style
  - Best use cases
  - Available customizations
  - Price tier table
- This is the page that turns browsers into buyers — they need to see and compare styles easily

### Pricing Page
- Full pricing breakdown by style category
- What's included at every tier
- Add-on pricing
- FAQ (MOQ, timeline, samples, rush options)
- "No hidden fees" banner

### Gallery
- Product photos organized by style
- Client work (with permission)
- Before/after: concept → finished product
- Behind-the-scenes factory shots

### Process Page
Simple visual timeline:
1. Submit your project → We get back to you within 24 hours
2. Design & mockup → We create a digital mockup for your approval
3. Sample → We send you a physical sample (included in your order)
4. Production → 30 days in our factory
5. Shipping → 10-15 days to your door
6. Delivered → Your custom bags arrive, ready to go

### Contact
- Form (same as homepage)
- Email: hello@alongway.com
- Phone (optional — depends on whether Easton wants a separate line)
- "Based in Los Angeles, CA. Made in partnership with our trusted manufacturing partner."

## Phase 2: Expansion (Post-Launch)

Once the business has traction:

- **Online ordering/cart** — Let customers build and order directly (quantity + customizations → cart → checkout)
- **Customer portal** — Order tracking, reorder, design files
- **Blog** — Custom bag inspiration, client stories, industry content (SEO play)
- **Lookbook** — Seasonal collections, trending styles, inspiration boards
- **Referral program** — Discount for referring other brands
- **Sample kit** — Order a physical sample kit with fabric swatches + mini tote
