# Alongway — Pricing Framework

_Updated: 2026-04-11 based on Easton's input_

## Pricing Philosophy

Transparent. On the website. No "request a quote" for standard orders.

Each style has a **base price** at a standard fabric weight. Then the customer adds upgrades:
- Heavier fabric → +$X
- Premium fabric → +$X
- Add-ons (pockets, patches, extra labels, etc.) → +$X each

**The customer should know their total cost in 60 seconds.**

## Order Minimums

- **50 pieces minimum per style**
- **100 pieces minimum per order**
- Mix and match styles to hit 100
- This opens the door to smaller businesses (coffee shops, events, startups) who don't need 100 of one bag but would order 50 of two styles

## Revenue Modeling

### Scenario 1: Small Coffee Shop
- 50 Carry Totes (10oz) at $9.50 = $475
- 50 Mini Totes (10oz) at $8.00 = $400
- **Total order: $875**
- Est. CGS: ~$400
- **Gross margin: ~54%**

### Scenario 2: Mid-Size Brand
- 250 Shoulder Totes (16oz) at $6.50 + $1.00 = $7.50 × 250 = $1,875
- Add back print (+$1.25 × 250) = $312.50
- Add contrast straps (+$0.50 × 250) = $125
- **Total order: $2,312.50**
- Est. CGS: ~$1,050
- **Gross margin: ~55%**

### Scenario 3: Premium Corporate Gift
- 100 Weekenders at $22.00 = $2,200
- 100 Dopp Kits at $12.00 = $1,200
- Add leather patch on both (+$2.00 × 200) = $400
- **Total order: $3,800**
- Est. CGS: ~$1,700
- **Gross margin: ~55%**

### Scenario 4: Volume Event Order
- 1,000 Essential Totes at $2.75 = $2,750
- Add second print color (+$0.75 × 1000) = $750
- **Total order: $3,500**
- Est. CGS: ~$1,800
- **Gross margin: ~49%**
