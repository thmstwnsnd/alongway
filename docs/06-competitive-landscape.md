# Alongway — Competitive Landscape

## The Three Lanes (And Where Alongway Fits)

### Lane 1: Blank Tote Sellers (Decorate Yourself)
These companies sell generic blank totes. Customer buys blanks, then finds a separate printer for decoration.

**AS Colour**
- Premium blank apparel/bags brand (Australia-based, US operations)
- Tote bags: $8-12 wholesale for undecorated blanks
- Limited styles: basic tote, shopper, small carry
- No customization beyond choosing a blank color
- Customer must source their own decoration
- By the time you add printing: $15-20+ per unit
- **Gap:** No custom anything. Just blanks.

**BAGGU**
- Consumer brand, not B2B custom
- Sells to end consumers directly
- Wholesale program exists but it's BAGGU-branded products, not custom
- $12-15 per bag at wholesale
- **Gap:** You can't put your brand on a BAGGU (it IS the brand)

**Eco Bags / Eco Totes**
- Budget blank totes, often organic/recycled materials
- $3-8 wholesale depending on quality
- Decoration is separate
- Quality varies significantly
- **Gap:** Cheap blanks, no custom manufacturing, decoration is your problem

**Liberty Bags**
- Wholesale blank tote distributor
- Canvas, nylon, cotton totes at $2-6 wholesale
- No decoration, no customization
- Minimum order: typically 1 unit (just blanks)
- **Gap:** Pure commodity blanks. Zero customization support.

### Lane 2: Overseas Factory-Direct (Alibaba, etc.)
Brands go directly to Chinese/overseas factories for fully custom bags.

**Alibaba / Global Sources Factories**
- Hundreds of factories offering custom totes
- Pricing: $1.50-5.00 per unit depending on complexity and MOQ
- MOQ: typically 500-3,000 units
- Timeline: 30-60 days production + 30-45 days shipping
- **Gaps:**
  - No brand, no trust, no guidance
  - Quality is a gamble
  - Communication barriers
  - Complex shipping/customs process
  - No pre-production samples (or expensive ones)
  - MOQ too high for small brands
  - If something goes wrong, you're on your own

**Key insight:** This is where Alongway's supply chain lives (Emily/HB Findo), but we're the brand layer, quality control, and concierge on top of the factory. The customer gets factory-direct pricing with a US-based partner managing everything.

### Lane 3: Promo / Merch Companies (Totes as an Afterthought)
Full-service promo companies that sell everything. Totes are one of 500 product categories.

**4imprint**
- Massive promo distributor. $1.2B+ revenue.
- Custom totes: $3-15+ per unit (wildly variable)
- Hundreds of tote options but mostly cheap promo-grade
- Decoration: basic screen print, usually 1-2 colors
- **Gap:** Volume promo, not quality. The "Costco of branded stuff." No design guidance, no premium options, no personality.

**CustomInk**
- Consumer-facing custom merch platform
- Totes: $8-25+ depending on style and decoration
- Online design tool (upload your logo, drag and drop)
- Quality: decent but not custom-manufactured
- Using pre-existing blanks, not made-to-order
- **Gap:** Still blank-based decoration. You're choosing from their catalog of blanks. Limited fabric, no custom construction.

**Swag.com**
- Tech-focused merch platform
- Totes: $8-20+ per unit
- Clean website, good UX
- But still just decorated blanks from their catalog
- **Gap:** Great website, mediocre product. Logo on a generic bag.

**Printful / Printify (Print-on-Demand)**
- Dropship / print-on-demand totes
- $10-15 per unit (includes printing)
- No minimums (1 unit)
- Limited styles, limited quality
- **Gap:** Convenience over quality. Good for testing designs, bad for branded merchandise.

**Stran Promotional Solutions**
- Full-service promo company
- Custom totes available but buried in massive product catalog
- Higher-touch service than 4imprint but not bag specialists
- **Gap:** Generalist, not specialist.

### Lane 4: Niche Custom Manufacturers (Closest Competitors)

**Tapacho Mfg** (HATS — not bags, but the MODEL)
- Single product: custom headwear
- 50-unit minimum, all-in pricing ($7.50-13.50)
- Clean website, clear process, strong IG presence
- No hidden fees, free shipping
- Pre-production sample included
- **THIS is the model Alongway follows, but for bags**

**Bulldog Bag Co.**
- Custom bags (various types)
- Higher-end, more complex bags (backpacks, duffles, laptop bags)
- Higher MOQs (typically 200-500+)
- More corporate/enterprise focused
- **Gap:** Too complex, too high-end, not focused on totes specifically

**Apolis (Global Citizen)**
- Custom market totes (Bangladesh-made)
- Known for the "Apolis Market Bag" with custom text
- Premium pricing: $20-30+ per unit
- Limited customization (basically just text on their standard bag)
- Ethical manufacturing angle
- **Gap:** One style, very expensive, limited customization. Beautiful product but not a custom manufacturer — more of a branded product company.

**Merchology**
- Corporate merch platform
- Totes available among many product categories
- $15-35 per unit (premium positioning)
- North Face, Patagonia, and other brand-name blanks with custom decoration
- **Gap:** Premium blank decoration, not custom manufacturing. Expensive because you're paying for the blank brand.

## The Opportunity

Nobody occupies the intersection of:
- ✅ Fully custom manufactured (not blanks)
- ✅ Tote/bag specialist (not a generalist)
- ✅ Low MOQ (50 units)
- ✅ All-in transparent pricing
- ✅ Simple, guided process
- ✅ Fast turnaround (~6 weeks)
- ✅ Great website and brand presence

That's the gap. That's Alongway.

## Key Takeaways for Positioning

1. **"We're not a promo company."** — Alongway makes retail-quality custom bags. Not cheap totes with a logo.
2. **"We're not Alibaba."** — We handle everything. You don't need to navigate overseas factories, customs, or quality control.
3. **"We're not a blank seller."** — Every bag is made from scratch. Your fabric, your design, your bag.
4. **"We do one thing, and we do it right."** — Tapacho proved this works for hats. Alongway proves it for bags.
5. **Price anchor:** We're cheaper than Apolis, Merchology, CustomInk, and most promo companies — and the product is fully custom, not a decorated blank.
