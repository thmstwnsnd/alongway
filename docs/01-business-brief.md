# Alongway — Business Brief

_"Custom Bags, Made Simple."_

## The Idea

Alongway is a custom tote and bag manufacturer that offers ~10 curated silhouettes with streamlined, all-in pricing. Customers pick a style, choose their fabric, customize everything (print, embroidery, labels, straps), and get a fully branded product delivered in ~6 weeks. No blanks. No inventory. Everything made to order.

## Why This Works

### The Gap
There are three players in the custom tote space right now:

1. **Blank sellers** (AS Colour, BAGGU wholesale, eco-totes) — Buy a generic blank, decorate it yourself. $8-12 for the blank alone. By the time you add printing, you're at $15-20+ for a basic tote with a logo slapped on it. No customization beyond the print.

2. **Alibaba/factory-direct** — Confusing, no guidance, inconsistent quality, language barriers, unclear pricing, high MOQs (500-1000+). You're on your own.

3. **Full-service promo companies** — Expensive, slow, totes are an afterthought. They sell everything (pens, mugs, lanyards, totes, whatever) and nothing is their specialty.

**Nobody is doing what Tapacho Mfg did for hats — but for bags.**

A clean, branded experience where you:
- Pick your silhouette
- Choose your fabric and color
- Customize everything (straps, stitch, pockets, labels)
- Get a fixed, all-in price
- Receive your order in ~6 weeks

That's the gap. That's Alongway.

### Why It'll Work
- **Totes are universal.** Every brand, every event, every company needs them. It's the most ordered custom product category alongside tees and hats.
- **The supply chain exists.** Emily at HB Findo already makes our totes. Proven quality, proven relationship, proven pricing.
- **Simple product, high perceived customization.** A tote is: fabric + handles + decoration + label. Four variables. But those variables create massive perceived value for the customer.
- **Great margins.** Emily's DDP cost: $2.77-3.95 per unit. Market pricing: $7-15+. That's 40-60% margins at scale.
- **Low complexity = scalable.** One product category, one factory, streamlined process. This doesn't need a huge team.
- **Riches in niches.** Tapacho proved it with hats. Single product focus = clear brand, clear messaging, clear ads, clear IG presence.

## Business Model

### Structure
- **DBA** under Orange Collective LLC (same EIN, QuickBooks, seller's permit)
- **Sister company** to Orange Goods — cross-referral relationship
- Separate brand, website, Instagram, identity
- Shared CRM infrastructure (HubSpot)

### Revenue Model
- Made-to-order custom bags
- All-in pricing (includes: fabric, construction, 1-color decoration, woven label, shipping, setup)
- Revenue from add-ons (contrast fabric, extra labels, patches, back print, pockets, rush production)
- No inventory risk, no warehouse overhead

### Factory
- **HB Findo (Emily)** — Primary manufacturer (China)
- Same factory that produces OG hats
- 30 days production + 10-15 days shipping (sea freight standard)
- Existing relationship, trusted quality

### MOQ
- **50 units** (accessible tier for small brands, startups, events)
- **100 units** (standard tier, best price breaks begin)
- Volume discounts at 250, 500, 1000+

## Target Customer

### Primary
- **Small-to-mid brands** (DTC, retail, food & bev, hospitality) that want custom merch but don't have a merch team
- **Event planners** — conferences, trade shows, corporate retreats
- **Marketing teams** at mid-size companies that need branded bags for campaigns, gifting, or retail

### Secondary
- **Startups** launching branded merch for the first time
- **Nonprofits** for fundraising or awareness campaigns
- **Boutiques and retailers** wanting private-label bags

### Customer Profile
- Orders 50-500 units
- Budget: $500-5,000 per order
- Wants quality but doesn't want to deal with overseas factories directly
- Values a simple, clear process over endless options
- Needs it in 6 weeks, not 12
