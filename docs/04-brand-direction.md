# Alongway — Brand Direction

## Name
**Alongway**
- "Along" — a journey, a companion, going the distance
- "way" — direction, path, how you get there
- Together: "We go with you."
- Every bag has a journey — make it one that takes you a long way
- Domain: alongway.co
- Instagram: @alongway (check availability)

## Core Message

**"We set out to make the best and most customizable totes through a simple, fast, affordable, and fully branded process. There are plenty of totes out there. None like Alongway."**

### The Four Pillars
1. **Simple** — Pick your style, choose your fabric, done. Pricing is on the website.
2. **Fast** — 30 days production, shipped to your door.
3. **Affordable** — All-in pricing, no setup fees, no hidden charges, shipping included.
4. **Fully Branded** — Not a blank with a logo. Your fabric, your color, your print, your label. Every detail is yours.

## Tagline Options
1. **"Custom Bags, Made Simple."** — The frontrunner. Says exactly what we do and how we do it.
2. **"Made for the long way."** — Brand-line tagline. Speaks to durability, quality, and the journey. Pairs perfectly with the name.
3. **"Plenty of totes. None like Alongway."** — Confident. Memorable. Works great on ads and IG.
4. **"Your Bag, Made."** — Shorter, more brand-y. Works well as a secondary tagline.
5. **"There are plenty of tote bags out there. But not like this."** — Longer version for website hero or about page.

**Recommended use:**
- Hero/primary: *"Custom Bags, Made Simple."* — clear, functional, explains the business
- Brand line / emotional: *"Made for the long way."* — goes on packaging, labels, IG bio, about page

## Brand Personality

### What Alongway Is
- **Confident but approachable.** We know bags. We don't need to shout about it.
- **Fun but serious about the product.** The vibe is "let's make something cool" not "let's fill out a purchase order."
- **Simple and clear.** No jargon, no confusion, no 47-page catalog. Pick. Customize. Done.
- **Quality-obsessed.** Retail-quality, not promo-grade. These are bags people want to keep.
- **LA energy.** Warm, creative, direct. Not corporate. Not startup-bro. Just good people making good bags.

### What Alongway Is NOT
- Generic promo company
- Cheap/budget/fast-fashion
- Corporate or stiff
- Complicated or confusing
- "Just another tote company"

### Voice Examples

**Yes:**
- "Pick your style. Choose your fabric. We handle the rest."
- "No hidden fees. No setup costs. Just custom bags, made right."
- "50 units or 5,000 — same quality, same process, same team."

**No:**
- "Revolutionizing the custom bag space with innovative solutions"
- "Leverage our industry-leading manufacturing capabilities"
- "Premium promotional tote bag solutions for your business needs"

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Something with character — slightly rounded, confident, not too serious. Think: something between a sans-serif and a display font.
  - References: General Sans, Satoshi, Clash Display, or something custom
- **Body:** Clean sans-serif — Inter, Figtree (like OG), or similar
- The logo wordmark could be custom lettering that becomes the brand signature

### Logo Direction

**Wordmark-forward:**
- "Alongway" as a clean wordmark is the strongest play
- Can be styled with a subtle bag reference (handle in a letterform, stitching detail)
- Needs to work at all sizes: IG avatar, woven label (tiny), website header (large)

**Icon/Mark:**
- Simple tote silhouette that becomes recognizable at small sizes
- Could be abstract — two vertical lines (handles) + a curved bottom
- The icon should work as an IG profile pic on its own

**What to Avoid:**
- Overly complex illustrations
- Clipart-looking bag icons
- Script fonts (reads feminine, limits the audience)
- Anything that looks like a promo company logo

### Photography/Visual Style
- **Product shots:** Clean, well-lit, lifestyle context (hands holding bags, bags in real settings)
- **Flat lays:** Bag + contents spilling out (shows scale, utility)
- **Process shots:** Factory behind-the-scenes, fabric samples, printing, stitching
- **Mockups:** High-quality digital mockups for pre-sale visualization
- **Color story:** Warm, natural light, earthy backgrounds, not sterile white-box studio
- **IG Grid:** Alternating product shots, lifestyle, process, client features

### Packaging/Unboxing
- Bags ship flat in a branded poly mailer or kraft box
- Include a branded card/insert: care instructions + "Made for [Client Brand]"
- Sticker or woven label on packaging
- Keep it simple — the bag IS the product, packaging supports, doesn't overshadow
