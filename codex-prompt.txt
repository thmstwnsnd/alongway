Build a complete Next.js marketing website for "Alongway" — a premium custom tote & bag brand.

## Brand
- Name: Alongway (one word)
- Tagline: "Made to carry."
- Confidence line: "The finest bags in all the land."
- Emotional hook: "For now, for later."
- Colors: Bone #EEE6D2, Light Bone #F2ECE2, Blue #364FA0, Orange #EB4628, Charcoal #262626
- Font: Use Google Font "Figtree" as primary font
- Design reference: Clean, bold, product-forward like graza.co. Lots of white space. Warm and premium but approachable.

## Pages to Build

### 1. Home (/)
- Hero: Full-width, large headline "Made to carry." with subline "The finest bags in all the land." CTA button: "See the Collection" (links to /collection). Use a placeholder gradient or solid color section (Bone bg, Charcoal text) — no real images needed yet.
- Brief "What is Alongway?" blurb: 2-3 sentences about curated bag silhouettes, all-in pricing, factory-direct quality.
- Featured bags: 3 placeholder bag cards (names: The Market, The Daily, The Weekender) with placeholder colored boxes instead of images.
- "How It Works" section: 4 steps — Pick your bag, Share your artwork, We handle production, Delivered to your door
- CTA strip: "Ready to start?" with button linking to /start

### 2. Collection (/collection)
- Grid of 10 bag silhouettes with placeholder colored boxes
- Bag names: The Market, The Daily, The Weekender, The Pocket, The Zip Tote, The Canvas Shopper, The Mini, The Drawstring, The Crossbody, The Boat Bag
- Each card: bag name, one-liner description, "Starting at $X" (use $8.50 for small bags, $12.50 for medium, $16.50 for large), link to /collection/[slug]

### 3. Individual Bag Page (/collection/[slug])
- Dynamic route for each bag
- Placeholder image box
- Name, description, materials (placeholder: "Premium canvas, reinforced stitching")
- Dimensions (placeholder)
- MOQ: 50 units
- Pricing tier table: 50 / 100 / 250 / 500 units with placeholder per-unit prices
- CTA: "Start Your Order" linking to /start

### 4. Pricing (/pricing)
- Intro: "No quotes. No surprises. Just clear pricing."
- Pricing table for all 10 bags across 4 quantity tiers (50/100/250/500)
- Use placeholder numbers, clearly marked "Starting from"
- Note: "Prices include production, standard customization, and shipping to one US address. Setup fee: $50 one-time."

### 5. How It Works (/how-it-works)
- Expanded 4-step process with emoji icons and longer descriptions
- FAQ section: 5-6 common questions (MOQ, turnaround, artwork formats, customization, reorders)

### 6. Start Your Order (/start)
- Clean form: Bag style dropdown (all 10 bags), Quantity, Timeline, Artwork ready yes/no, Name, Email, Company, Notes
- On submit: show a thank-you message (static, no backend needed yet)

### 7. About (/about)
- Brand story: "We built Alongway because good bags should not require a sourcing director."
- What sets us apart: curated silhouettes, all-in pricing, factory-direct quality, built to be kept, brand feel
- Contact: hello@alongway.co
