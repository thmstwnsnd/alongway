Below the pricing table, add a "Configure your order" section with:

**Fabric selector:**
- Show all fabrics from data/fabrics.ts grouped by tier
- Starter fabrics: selectable, no upcharge shown

- Default: 12oz Cotton Canvas selected

**Live price calculator:**
